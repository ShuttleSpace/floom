## Notes

- Items 1, 2, 4, 7 and 8 are recorded with evidence rather than left blank: "it compiled" is not a gate result, and this is the first time this backend has been *run* at all — the ten `docs/ios.md` steps (1–10, plus 8b/9b) were all compile-only until this pass.
- **Input injection is the reason this took a tool install.** `xcrun simctl` has no tap/type/keystroke command (only `io` media, `ui` appearance, `pbcopy/pbpaste`, `openurl`), and AppleScript GUI automation is blocked by TCC (`-1743 …未获得授权将 Apple 事件发送给 System Events`). `idb_companion` was already installed; the missing piece was the `fb-idb` client, installed into a venv (`~/.local/share/idb-venv`). The `idb ui *` verbs are what made items 1/2/7 drivable; `simctl` alone was enough for item 4. Lesson: for iOS the *observability* tools are `simctl`, and the *input* tools are `idb` — neither alone covers a gate.
- **CJK glyphs.** The one real *rendering* defect found: Latin draws, Han does not. `你好世界` (preloaded, no input involved) and the five characters committed through the pinyin IME all render as tofu — so this is the text stack, not the IME: `parley`/`fontique` finds no Han-capable face on iOS. OHOS had the same class of problem and solved it by scanning `System/fonts` and registering the family (`crates/floom/src/platform/ohos_font.rs`, gated `target_env = "ohos"`, called from `FONT_CONTEXT` in `crates/floom/src/text/layout.rs`); iOS has no equivalent, and the simulator’s runtime does ship PingFang/Noto. A matching `target_os = "ios"` font-registration path is the likely fix and is the next iOS item — it is *not* claimed here.
- **a11y geometry.** The labels exist and cross into the OS a11y tree, but the frames are all zero — the neutral a11y layer is not forwarding layout bounds to the iOS `UIAccessibilityElement`s. This is why item 3 is `fail` rather than "labels work": a label without a frame is unusable to VoiceOver.
- The tap coordinates were computed from the screenshot, not guessed: the `+`/`-`/field centres are `(156,415)`/`(246,415)`/`(201,454)` in **points** on a 402×874 screen (the screenshot is 1206×2622 px, so ÷3). Same lesson as the Android and OHOS runs: pixel coordinates are not click coordinates, and here `idb` speaks points.
